Find a book with Google Books and generate a ready-made
[{{Cite book}}](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template:Cite_book) or
[{{Citation}}](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Template:Citation) citation template for the English
Wikipedia. The tool automatically checks whether the authors have Wikipedia articles and creates
the appropriate links.
