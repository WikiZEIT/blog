## What is the Google Knowledge Graph?

The **Google Knowledge Graph** is a semantic database, a so-called [knowledge
graph](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Knowledge_graph), that gathers, organizes, and connects
information about the world into a logical network of relationships. Instead of analyzing entered
keywords as isolated strings of characters, Google interprets them as real-world objects – known as
entities (people, places, companies, events, or things). This enables the search engine to
understand the connections between them, allowing it to deliver direct and highly precise answers to
users' queries.

In practice, the Knowledge Graph is responsible for displaying the so-called Knowledge Panel in
search results – the distinctive box on the right side or at the top of the screen that contains key
facts, images, and data summarizing the searched term. From an SEO perspective, being present in the
Knowledge Graph is crucial for building brand authority (E-E-A-T), increasing online visibility, and
optimizing for voice search. For Google to include a brand or creator in its database, it is
necessary to implement structured data (Schema.org) on the website, maintain a consistent Google
Business Profile, and secure entries in trusted external data sources such as Wikidata or Wikipedia.
